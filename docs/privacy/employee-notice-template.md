# Employee Transparency & Monitoring Notice Template

> **Notice to Employers:** This document is a technical and informational template for employee transparency as required by **SPEC §2.1 and §11.3**. Organizations must have this notice reviewed by legal counsel in their relevant jurisdiction (e.g. DPDP Act in India, GDPR in the EU, or state privacy laws in the US).

---

## Notice of Laptop Productivity Monitoring

### 1. Purpose of Monitoring
Your employer, **[Organization Name]**, utilizes Laptop Productivity Monitoring software on company-issued laptops to:
- Track work hours, active time, and productivity metrics during scheduled work windows.
- Assist managers with balanced workload allocation and resource planning.
- Protect organizational information assets and ensure policy compliance.

### 2. What Data Is Collected
When monitoring is active, the desktop agent collects:
- **Foreground Applications:** Names of active applications and duration of usage.
- **Session State:** Whether the computer is active, idle, locked, or asleep.
- **Periodic Screenshots:** Intermittent screen captures taken at policy-defined intervals (e.g., every 5 minutes) during working hours.
- **System Telemetry:** Agent version, uptime, and sync status.

### 3. What Is NEVER Collected (Non-Negotiable)
Under our strict software safeguards:
- **NO Keylogging:** Keystrokes are never recorded or logged.
- **NO Password Capture:** Password managers and sensitive fields are excluded from capture.
- **NO Private Message Scraping:** Personal chat applications and private communications are excluded.
- **NO Remote Shell or Command Execution:** The monitoring software cannot run arbitrary commands or access local files.
- **NO Covert/Hidden Monitoring:** The agent tray icon is always visible, displaying monitoring status.

### 4. Employee Rights & Self-View Access
As an employee, you have the right to:
1. **View Your Own Data:** Access the Employee Self-View dashboard at any time to inspect your logged activity, screenshots, and metrics.
2. **Review Active Policy:** See the exact screenshot frequency, idle threshold, and allowed capture windows applied to your account.
3. **Submit Disputes:** If you believe an interval was misclassified or an erroneous capture occurred, you may submit a formal dispute directly through your dashboard.

### 5. Acknowledgement
By signing in and launching the software, you acknowledge receipt of this notice and understand the nature of company-issued laptop monitoring during work hours.
